About FTIR Vol-Cal

Overview

FTIR Vol-Cal is a tool for calculating dissolved H2O (wt%) and CO2 (ppm) concentrations in volcanic glass from FTIR transmission spectra. It replaces a suite of Excel spreadsheet templates with a single, reproducible workflow.

How It Works

  1. Thickness — Determined from interference fringes (manual count, direct input, or automatic detection from reflectance spectrum).
  2. Density & Refractive Index — Calculated from major oxide composition using the Church & Johnson (1980) method.
  3. H2O Peak — Linear baseline correction through 2200–2400 and 3700–3800 cm-1, peak height at 3550 cm-1.
  4. CO3 Fitting — Three models available for the carbonate doublet (~1430/1515 cm-1):
    • Simple least-squares (matches Excel CO2_bestfit.xls)
    • Least-squares with shifted peak positions
    • PCA baseline (following Shi et al. 2024, PyIRoGlass)
  5. Concentrations — Beer-Lambert law with molar absorptivities.

References

  • Shi, G. et al. (2024) PyIRoGlass. Volcanica, 7(2), 471–501. doi:10.30909/vol.07.02.471501
  • Church, B.N. & Johnson, W.M. (1980) Calculation of the refractive index of silicate glasses from chemical composition. GSA Bulletin, 91(10), 619–625.
